Our newest double edge safety razor! Magnets hold the blade cover in place.
Double sided razor.
The solid bar side has a neutral blade exposure for everyday use. The scalloped side has a positive blade exposure and larger blade gap for a closer shave.
CNC machined in the USA from lightweight aerospace aluminum with precision ground alignment pins that easily and accurately position the blade.
Our 'Stinger' design feature Honeycomb and Bee engraving that makes a unique and attractive presentation at your sink.

Razor Technical Details
Mild Side: 0.000" blade exposure, 0.026" blade gap
Medium Side: 0.006" blade exposure, 0.030" blade gap
N52 grade Neodymium magnets with 2.8 lbs. holding force
Locating dowel pins ground to 0.0002" accuracy
6061 T6 CNC machined body with Type III anodizing
40 grams
Recommended blades: Astra, 7 O'Clock, Treet, BIC
