# Wetshaving world knowledge base

Maintained by the standing learner agent; read by Martha and by the catalog
validators. Purpose: capture the lore of the wetshaving world that the
catalog work depends on — brand lineages and closures, product aliases and
naming collisions, collaborations, formats, stores, community conventions.

## Discipline
- Every fact carries: the claim, source(s), date learned, and a confidence
  tag: **established** (primary or strong corroborated source) or
  **lead** (single/community source, unverified — a pointer, not a fact).
- Only **established** facts may be used by validators as if true.
  Leads are chased, not trusted.
- The catalog is a source too (user ruling 2026-11-07): a **verified**
  catalog entry counts as a source — cite the entry id PLUS its underlying
  evidence (type and URL), never the entry's existence alone, so the chain
  always terminates at primary evidence. Corroborated, attested, and
  unverified entries are leads only.
- Append, don't rewrite: corrections are new dated entries that name what
  they supersede.
- Catalog-relevant facts get flagged **[catalog]** so the validators and
  Martha notice them.

## Files
- `lineages.md` — brand renames, closures, ownership, collaborations.
- `product_aliases.md` — products known by multiple names; names that
  mislead; community naming conventions.
- `world_notes.md` — formats, stores, events, conventions, people.
- `backlog.md` — open questions worth chasing (the learner works these
  down and adds new ones as curiosity strikes).
- `learning_log.md` — per-run record: what was investigated, what was
  found, what was added where.
