# (c4-narrow-210) B&M Hand & Body formula/size/price transition — evidence note
Learner run 2026-11-18 ~15:06–16:00 CST. Lead as framed 13:06: early line tallow
5.25 oz $5.99 (Nov 2020, Dickens bar) vs "current vegan 4.75 oz $11" — when did
the line switch, reformulation in place or replacement?

## Verdict: THREE staggered in-place changes, 2021–2024, per scent — no replacements

All on the SAME Shopify listings (same product ids, created 2020-05-25 / 2020-07-15);
bodies edited in place. Wayback `id_` replays read; formula judged by exact INCI
string counts ("Tallow (Beef)" / "Goat Milk Powder" / "Coconut Milk", each echoed
7x across JSON-LD + body + meta — a 0/7 flip is a real change, not rendering noise).

### Launch (established)
- Line launched 2020-05-25: Seville (id 4579766632501), Bay Rum (4579767779381),
  Barrister's Reserve Lavender (4579770695733). Cologne Russe added 2020-07-15
  (id 4626282348597). All TALLOW 5.25 oz $5.99 at launch — Seville 2020-09-25,
  Cologne Russe 2020-08-10, Bay Rum 2021-09-21 captures all tallow/$5.99/5.25 oz.
  Dickens Revisited bar (Nov 2020, tallow $5.99 5.25 oz) was the line's formula
  then, NOT a one-off batch.

## CORRECTIONS to the lead's framing [catalog]
1. "$11" is the NAKED ARMOR price, not the maker's. Maker store: $9.99 (13 listings,
   bo snapshot 2026-11-18; Aleppo $10.99). NA carries SEPARATE "(Vegan)" listings at
   $11.00 (11 listings: Lavender + Bay Rum created 2024-11-03; Seville + Spice +
   Lavanille 2024-12-15; Fougère Gothique 2025-08-24; LGC + Cologne Russe + Waves
   2026-02-17/18; Cheshire + Mûir(e) Wood 2026-09-29) — NA created new listings for
   the coconut-milk era rather than retitling; the maker kept the same handles.
   Never cite "$11" as the maker price.
2. "Vegan" applies ONLY from the coconut-milk switch (late 2024). The 2021–2024
   vegetable formula contained Goat Milk Powder — calling it vegan is wrong.
3. STALE-COPY TRAP: the maker's live "The Soap" block still says "goat milk powder"
   in the prose while its own INCI lists Coconut Milk. The INCI governs; never cite
   the copy's "goat milk powder" for current bars.
4. The transition has NO single date — four changes, each staggered per scent over
   ~3 years. Any Hand & Body catalog entry needs era handling: tallow-era bars
   (2020–2021/23, per scent) vs vegetable+goat (2021/23–2024) vs coconut-milk vegan
   (late 2024+) are different formulas on the same listing.

## Method note
- A bare plain-text "tallow" hit on a product page is NOT formula evidence: the
  Lavender 2022 captures contain "tallow" only inside a customer review ("previous
  tallow bar soap formulation"). Judge formula by the exact INCI strings and their
  counts, never by substring search.
- Sale prices ($3.99 Bay Rum Jan 2022, $4.49 Cologne Russe Sep 2023) both fall in the
  pre-switch window for their scent — possible tallow-stock clearance; lead only.
